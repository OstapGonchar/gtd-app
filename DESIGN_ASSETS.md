# Q2 Focus - Design Assets Brief

## Design Direction
**Style:** Modern minimalist with subtle depth, glassmorphism accents, soft gradients
**Mood:** Calm productivity, focused energy, premium feel
**Inspiration:** Linear, Raycast, Arc Browser, Notion

---

## Color Palette

### Light Theme (Day: 6am-6pm)
- Background: `#F8FAFC` (soft white)
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with subtle shadow
- Text Primary: `#1E293B`
- Text Secondary: `#64748B`

### Dark Theme (Night: 6pm-6am)
- Background: `#0A0A0F` (deep space black)
- Surface: `#12121A` with glow effects
- Text Primary: `#F1F5F9`
- Text Secondary: `#94A3B8`

### Accent Colors (Quadrants)
- Q1 Urgent/Important: `#F43F5E` (rose) - fire, intensity
- Q2 Important/Not Urgent: `#10B981` (emerald) - growth, life
- Q3 Urgent/Not Important: `#F59E0B` (amber) - caution, yield
- Q4 Not Urgent/Important: `#6B7280` (slate) - fade, minimize

## Animation Concepts

### Task Logged Success
- Brief confetti of emerald particles for Q2 tasks
- Subtle pulse for other quadrants
- Duration: 300ms

### Streak Increase
- Fire icon grows briefly, particles rise
- Number counter animates up
- Duration: 500ms

### Day Complete (All tasks logged)
- Soft glow around the day in calendar
- Gentle celebration, not overwhelming

## Component Styling Notes

### Cards/Surfaces
- Border radius: 16px (large), 12px (medium), 8px (small)
- Shadow (light): `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`
- Shadow (dark): `0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)` + subtle glow

### Buttons
- Primary: Filled with gradient
- Secondary: Outline with hover fill
- Border radius: 12px
- Height: 48px (large), 40px (medium), 32px (small)

### Inputs
- Background slightly darker/lighter than surface
- Focus: Emerald border glow
- Border radius: 12px
